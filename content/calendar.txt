title: Open weight LLMs for mathematicians
speaker: Olivier Verdier
series: Seminar
date: 2026-10-20
time: 15:15-17:15
location: KTH, Lindstedtsvägen 25, room D2
abstract: A presentation of open weight models, open source orchestrators, and how to use them to do mathematics. I'll give some recommendation as to what subscription to take, which orchestrator and which models to use for mathematics. We'll talk about privacy concerns, how to keep up with the latest LLMs, possible censorship. I'll also touch upon dependent types and theorem proving with LLMs.

title: Lean for the Working Mathematician
speaker: Isabel Dahlgren, Hampus Nyberg, Yaël Dillies
series: Seminar
date: 2026-09-28
time: 15:15-16:15
location: KTH, Lindstedtsvägen 25, room D2
abstract: Two years ago, formalising elementary statements in Lean used to be rather tedious. Today, we can formalise much research-level mathematics with a basic AI subscription. This creates new use cases of Lean, making it a practical tool for the working mathematician. I will begin with an introduction to Lean, Mathlib and related tools (Palomar, TauCeti, Verso), and then discuss possible ways to use Lean for research. No prior Lean experience will be assumed. Welcome!
